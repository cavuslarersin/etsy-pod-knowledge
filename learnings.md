- **VISIBILITY: ranking fundamentals & Star Seller.** Reported 2026 algorithm weighting: Query matching ~40%, Listing Quality ~35% (conversion/CTR/recency/reviews), Customer & Market Experience ~25% (rating/shipping/case-rate/completeness); search is semantic/intent-based and meaningfully personalized per-shopper — confirmed at exec level on Etsy's Oct 28 2026 Q3 call as a real, growing GMS driver (+~2pp YoY from personalization tuning). New-listing recency boost ~14-21 days + separate 7-14 day post-renewal testing window; 6+ months inactivity loses ~3%/month (light periodic edits help); description length (~300-500 words, keyword in first 100-150) is an explicit ranking input. In-place editing (title/photo/price on an active listing) is free and does NOT reset recency/age or trigger the $0.20 fee — only a true relist/renewal does, and a multi-quantity listing hides a $0.20 fee + 4-month reset on every unit sold. Oct 1 2026 shipping-visibility rule (still live): US-domestic shipping ≥$6 reduces visibility (exemptions: digital, non-US, items >$35 w/ $35+ free-shipping option, calculated-shipping); 5+ high-quality photos now needed for full visibility. Auto-renewal has been gated on complete shipping/delivery-estimate fields since Sept 22 2026 — a listing missing them goes invisible on expiry until manually fixed (audit both shops periodically). Title guidance: keyword-stuffing is now framed as actively harmful, not just unhelpful — state the item once, keep titles under 15 words, move subjective/marketing words and all gift/occasion phrasing into the 13 tag slots. AI-disclosure text should sit near the top of a description, not the bottom (bottom-placement reportedly correlates with more frequent audits). Star Seller (eased Aug 1 2026, reassessed monthly/rolling 3mo): ≥5 completed orders (~$300), 95%+ response-within-24h, 95%+ on-time shipping, ≥4.8 rating, first sale ≥90 days — ties to featured-placement odds per the Handbook, clears any Payment Reserve within 24h. Shop rating itself is lifetime+recency-weighted (eff. Mar 13 2026) but Star Seller keeps its strict 3-month window.
