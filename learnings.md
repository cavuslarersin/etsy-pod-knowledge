## 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-14 (research passes 1–18, consolidated 2026-09-29 to control file length; no facts dropped — Star Seller thresholds below reflect the Aug 2026 easing already noted in the 09-23 entry, and the duplicate ecommercebytes closure note is merged into one mention)
- **VISIBILITY: ranking fundamentals.** Buyer-behavior signals (CTR, add-to-cart, purchase completion) weigh heavily alongside keyword relevance (title > tags > category/attributes); front-load concrete traits (color/material/size/occasion) in titles, keep subjective filler out, soft ~70-char title cap before reading as stuffed. Full About section + Shop Policies + review volume/quality is an explicit ranking input, not just trust. Reported 2026 algorithm weighting: Query matching ~40%, Listing Quality ~35% (conversion/CTR/recency/reviews), Customer & Market Experience ~25% (rating/shipping speed/completeness) — a damaged CX score (open cases/DMCA/violations) drags down every listing shop-wide. Search matching is semantic/intent-based (e.g. "cozy gift for new mom" surfaces a blanket with none of those words) — write around the gift-occasion phrase a buyer would type, not keyword-repeat. New-listing recency boost window is ~14-21 days, separate from a 7-14 day "testing window" post-renewal (concentrate promo push across the full window). Listing "staleness": 6+ months total inactivity loses ~3% ranking/month — keep a periodic light-edit schedule (tag/alt-text/description tweak), not just renewal. Free-shipping's ranking boost has moderated in 2026 vs. earlier years; delivery-date speed (on-time-ship history + carrier transit data) is its own ranking factor distinct from the shipping-price penalty, and only complete/accurate shipping profiles qualify for the delivery-date search filter at all.
- **VISIBILITY: Star Seller.** Gates an actual search filter (non-Star-Seller shops vanish when a shopper toggles it on) and is tied to featured-placement odds (Handbook: badge holders get "increased chance of being featured in Etsy buyer marketing"); reassessed the 1st of each month over a rolling 3-month window. Current (eased Aug 1, 2026) thresholds: ≥5 completed orders (≈$300, down from 10), 95%+ response-within-24h (per conversation thread, not per message), 95%+ on-time shipping w/tracking, ≥4.8 avg rating (4-star reviews weighted less harshly since the easing), first sale ≥90 days old; the badge clears any active Payment Reserve within 24h.
- **VISIBILITY: featured placement & content.** Editors' Picks has no application/fee — curated on About-section completeness (brand origin/process + workspace photos/video) and hero photography per the Handbook's "Tips for Getting Featured"; more active listings means more editor-discovery chances. Mobile is 70%+ of searches and auto-plays listing video while scrolling — video-equipped listings reportedly get prioritized. Off-site traffic (Pinterest/TikTok/IG/blogs) is a demand-and-legitimacy signal boosting internal ranking, not just a separate channel — Pinterest is the best fit (evergreen pins, purchase intent). A separate "Getting Your Products Into Holiday Gift Guides" Handbook article covers external press/media gift guides — pitch editors directly, target the gift recipient not the product, offer samples, stock inventory first; pet-portrait gifts are a natural fit.
- **VISIBILITY: tools.** AI "Listing Title" tool (since Sept 15, 2026) reads title/photo/description, bulk-editable — review each suggestion before accepting. Search Visibility dashboard: "listing prioritization" ranks which listings would most benefit from a refresh (work down that list rather than refreshing at random) + per-listing impressions/CTR; shipping-price suppression reason-code (US-domestic ≥$6, bulk-editable); "Attribute Suggestions" (still Wall Hangings/Prints only in this window); beta "Real-Time Quality Feedback." Printify's "7-10 day freeze rule": don't panic-edit title/tags after a visibility drop (forces re-indexing, wipes conversion history) — wait 7-10 days and check processing time first (the one fully seller-controlled ranking factor). Photo cap 10→20 images (Aug 2025, still under-used) — check listings use more than ~5-10 of 20 slots.
- **VISIBILITY: Ads & new channels.** Etsy Ads "Listing Strategies" ($25+/day min): per-listing goal of Greater visibility / Lower click cost / Efficient spending; hold a strategy 30 days before switching. POD scaling guidance: start $1-3/day, scale 25-50% only after 30 days of sustained ROAS, target ROAS 3.5-4.5x, extra spend to highest dollar-margin listings, fix organic conversion under 1.5% before raising spend; three cohorts (Always-On Winners funded continuously, Growth Cohort 30-100 designs 2-4wk, Testing Cohort new designs rotated monthly). Ad attribution model changes Oct 4, 2026: old model credited a sale only on same-item-within-30-days or any-item-within-24h; new model credits any item in the shop bought within a full 30 days — expect more revenue reading as ad-attributed, re-baseline post-Oct-4. Etsy's native ChatGPT app (May 2026 beta) exposes the full catalog to natural-language shopping, 20%+ of referral traffic now from AI conversations — optimize with natural/semantic phrasing, a photo readable at small thumbnail size, explicit who-it's-for/occasion language; ties to Offsite Ads (opt-out, most shops enrolled by default) — don't opt out if this channel matters.
- **VISIBILITY: timing tactics.** Reorder shop sections so the most seasonal one leads (Halloween first in Sept, Christmas by Nov). Time a manual renewal 1-2h before a sale/coupon goes live so the recency boost stacks. Rotate 2-3 tags on top-visibility listings every 7-10 days through Q4; land Q4/holiday keywords by mid-Oct. Publish new listings Tue-Thu 9am-2pm EST (weekday morning peak) in addition to the 6-9pm local evening peak (+15-30% visibility in first 48h vs. random-time); seasonal listings need 3-4 weeks lead time, live by Oct 1 at the latest. Use 2600×2000 landscape source images so the automatic square-thumbnail crop doesn't cut off design elements. Descriptions are scanned for keyword/query matching too — work gift-occasion phrasing into the first couple of sentences.
- **VISIBILITY: Printify features.** Pop-Up Store Collections + a "Drop model" guide for scarcity/limited-time launches, a relaunched Premium positioned as a bundled "POD growth toolkit," catalog additions (windbreakers, work jackets, quarter-zip pullovers, soft-shell vests), TikTok Shop (US) integration. Native mockups from the real print file remain the default photo source — Placeit tested unreliable for exact product-shape/silhouette matching (wrong product types even on scoped searches); Printify's REST API has no mockup-generation/Placeit endpoint — template selection stays the one genuinely browser-only step.
- **API/implementation gotchas (Etsy Open API v3).** Shipping-profile creation needs `origin_postal_code` + per-destination `min/max_delivery_days` (copy from an existing profile via GET); verify the shop's actual shipping cost via the profile's `primary_cost` (`get_shipping_profiles`), not Printify's own cost-estimate table (a different number). Personalized listings use the inventory/offerings model — price changes need `PUT /listings/{id}/inventory` not PATCH, and the `offerings` payload must echo each existing `readiness_state_id` back or the request is rejected.
- **Operational notes (resolved incidents, kept for reference).** Reload rather than retry on a stuck browser page; Printify's mockup-angle-grid checkboxes can silently navigate away (use "Select all"). Periodically verify each listing's actual rank-1 cover photo (uploading a new image doesn't auto-replace rank-1 — must `DELETE` the old one) and that every listing still returns from `GET /listings/{id}`.
- **COMPLIANCE RISK: originality & AI disclosure.** The Creativity Standards' old "or using a templated design/pattern" carve-out is gone (eff. June 10, 2025) — a design must be the seller's own creation, not just used under a valid commercial license; extended to computerized/sublimation/laser-cut/embroidery production Aug 11, 2026 (confirmed via Prohibited Items Policy §2). AI-disclosure enforcement is live and actively removing POD listings (began Jan 14, 2026; ~12,000 removed/~8,500 warned Q1 2026) and covers generative-AI photo editing/retouching, not just AI-generated art (an audit found ~68% of sampled "AI-touched" listings, 400,000+, missing disclosure). Applied fix on UpCreateDesign: disclosure sentence appended to all 24 active listing descriptions.
- **COMPLIANCE RISK: IP/trademark & other.** Enforcement is increasingly automated/bulk-filed; two verified strikes within a year is a common path to permanent suspension — avoid any brand/character/sports-team reference, even "inspired by." Real-animal-fur ban (mink/fox/rabbit/badger/coyote/raccoon dog, no vintage exemption) eff. Aug 11, 2026 — not applicable to current mug/tumbler/apparel lines. Found and fixed a batch of 10 UpCreateDesign listings with `who_made` wrongly `someone_else` instead of `i_did` — worth a periodic cross-shop check; PATCH requires `who_made`+`when_made`+`is_supply` sent together. Non-US sellers must prepay US import tariffs via DDP shipping (eff. July 9, 2026) or lose Etsy Purchase Protection.
